# Query

Load this guide only to answer from consolidated operational knowledge.

## Boundaries

- Use only Markdown with a configured operational-content role as evidence. The configured context governs vocabulary and the optional local authoring reference governs representation; neither supplies operational facts.
- The optional intent map supplies points of consultation, not operational evidence, a procedure, or a guarantee of completeness. Ground answers exclusively in canonical operational content actually read, never in the existence of a route or its guidance.
- Do not inspect ingestion material, archived local sources, tickets, external URLs, YAML, D2, or agent instructions to complete an answer.
- Rendered diagrams may aid reading but never override or complete canonical Markdown.
- Do not change files except to produce an explicitly requested PDF, DOCX, SVG, or PNG through `export.md`.
- A gap remains a limitation. Do not fill it with general knowledge or unmarked inference.
- Query and read-only review do not require a grill.

## Navigate

1. Read the configured context file and root index, but not the optional local authoring reference.
2. Load the declared `navigation.intent_map` selectively when the question expresses a task or need without a known page or destination, or when indexes cannot locate the subject or leave uncertainty about which trunks to consult. A known destination keeps the existing path; do not load the map for every query.
3. A relevant route points to canonical pages or indexes to consult. Otherwise select only relevant top-level indexes, follow their page links and relevant internal references, and use text search when progressive navigation cannot locate the subject. An absent map, missing intent, or insufficient route returns to this existing path; do not invent coverage.
4. Stop when evidence is sufficient or cannot support a reliable answer.

## Route Opportunities

A concrete difficulty locating knowledge may justify signaling a route opportunity. During a query, only explain that recording it for future consultation is assisted indexing requiring editorial authority and human curation. Do not assume the reader has that authority or write access. The query does not authorize starting indexing, writing the map, creating tickets, or recording governance. Query must remain usable without write access or editorial authority.

## Answer

- Write in the configured instance language and preserve its canonical terms.
- Distinguish current operation, change proposals, pending questions, and limitations using the labels configured by the instance.
- End with a list of the wiki pages actually consulted, using their titles and relative links.
- Add a limitations section for insufficient, conflicting, or explicitly qualified evidence.
- Make a human-readable document self-contained and list provenance source pages at the end instead of scattering citations through its body.
